It helps users obtain information, understand unfamiliar topics, explore options, and receive guidance without needing to search for information manually or consult a person for every question.<br><br>**Beneficiaries:** Students, teachers, researchers, developers, businesses, and general users who need information or assistance with their tasks. |

| **3. Quality dimensions with measurement method** | **Dimension 1: Accuracy and factual consistency**<br><br>**What it evaluates:** Whether ChatGPT provides factually correct information and maintains consistency across all sessions.<br>**Measurement method:** We compared each response to check whether each of the four responses gives the correct answer and counted how many are correct.<br>**Observed result:** All four sessions identified Kuala Lumpur as Malaysia’s capital and Putrajaya as the federal administrative centre. This supports the accuracy of this particular question.<br><br>**Dimension 2: Knowledge-boundary transparency**<br>**What it evaluates:** Whether ChatGPT clearly explains its limitations when answering questions about very recent events.<br>**Measurement method:** Evaluate each response to the recent-events question against five criteria: mentions of potentially outdated knowledge, access to real-time information, information delays, source reliability, and limitations in source availability. Record which criteria each response addresses.<br>**Observed result:** All four sessions explained that ChatGPT may not always have the latest information. However, they differed in the limitations they mentioned and the level of detail provided. For example, Member 2 also explained that information about events such as elections and casualty figures can change over time.<br><br>**Dimension 3: Capability transparency and scope handling**<br>**What it evaluates:** Whether ChatGPT clearly distinguishes between providing assistance and performing an action directly.<br>**Measurement method:** Compare the four flight-booking responses and record whether each distinguishes finding or comparing flights, guiding the user through booking, and completing the purchase. Assess whether the limitations are communicated clearly.<br>**Observed result:** All four sessions offered help with flights, but Member 1 and Member 3 explicitly mentioned that ChatGPT might not be able to complete the purchase directly, while Members 2 and 4 asked for travel details without clearly stating this limitation upfront. This could cause users to misunderstand whether ChatGPT can actually complete a booking or only provide assistance. However, we did not test whether any session could complete a booking. |
| **4. What did the system do WELL? Give a specific example.** | ChatGPT provided clear, relevant, and easy-to-understand answers. It explained information in an organized way and adapted its responses to different questions.<br><br>**Example:** When asked about the capital of Malaysia, ChatGPT correctly answered Kuala Lumpur and clarified that Putrajaya is the federal administrative centre. |
| **5. What did it do POORLY? Give a specific example.** | ChatGPT sometimes gave general answers without checking the user's specific needs or providing supporting sources. Its answers may also be incomplete or outdated if it does not access current information.<br><br>**Example:** When asked whether Python is good, ChatGPT listed its advantages and limitations but did not ask what the user wanted to use Python for. Therefore, the answer might not fully address the user's needs. |
| **6. Responsible AI risks. Who is harmed and how?** | **Risk 1: Hallucination**<br>ChatGPT can produce fluent, confident answers that are factually wrong, such as invented references, wrong statistics, and outdated facts. Students who cite fabricated sources in assignments may be penalised for academic misconduct; general users may make decisions based on false information.<br><br>**Risk 2: Over-reliance and lack of transparency**<br>The interface does not show how confident the model is or where an answer came from if web search is used. Users may trust the output without verifying it, reducing critical thinking and spreading errors.<br><br>**Risk 3: Privacy**<br>Users may paste personal, confidential, API, or university data into the chat without realising how conversations may be stored or used. The user and any third parties whose data was shared may be affected, as well as organisations whose confidential information is leaked. |
| **7. If you could fix ONE thing, what and why?** | Add a visible confidence or source indicator on factual answers. For factual claims, ChatGPT should show whether the answer is grounded in a retrieved source (with a link) or generated from model memory, and flag low-confidence or time-sensitive answers such as "This may be outdated. Please verify with an official source". This directly reduces the hallucination risk and over-reliance, because users can immediately see which answers they need to double-check, improving both accuracy perception and transparency. |
