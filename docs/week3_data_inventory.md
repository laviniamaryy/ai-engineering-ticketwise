## 3. Data Type and Preparation Pathway

All three selected sources are PDF documents containing unstructured text. Therefore, the PDF/TXT document preparation pathway (Pathway A) was selected.

The documents were processed using pypdf. The preparation operations included PDF text extraction, whitespace cleaning, structure-aware record preparation, validation and prepared-data export.

The final preparation approach preserved the document structures relevant to TicketWise:

- **FAQ:** Question-and-answer records were created, with the document introduction retained as a separate record. This produced 22 FAQ question-and-answer records and 1 introduction record.
- **Ticketing Terms:** The extracted content was organised into 17 section-aware records, including the introduction.
- **Refund Policy:** The cleaned policy content was retained as 1 document-level record.

The final prepared dataset contained 41 records in total. This structure-aware approach was selected instead of relying only on fixed-size character chunks because it better preserved FAQ question-and-answer groupings and Ticketing Terms sections.

## 5. Initial Inspection Observations and Limitations

Initial inspection was performed in Google Colab using the pypdf library.

- All three PDF files were successfully accessed and inspected.
- Text previews were extracted from the first page of each PDF.
- Text was extracted from all eight pages, with no pages returning zero extracted characters.
- The FAQ preview contained ticket purchasing information, the Ticketing Terms preview contained purchase conditions, and the refund policy preview contained refund and cancellation conditions.
- Some PDF layout artefacts affected the extracted text organisation. For example, website navigation text appeared in the extracted content.
- Successful text extraction does not guarantee that the original document structure, formatting and reading order are perfectly preserved.

Further preparation and validation were completed in Part B and Part C. The final prepared dataset contained 41 non-empty records, with unique record IDs and source metadata. The Part C pipeline was also rerun successfully, and its source fingerprints, prepared-output fingerprints, output sizes and source-to-output mappings matched between runs.

The publication versions of S01 and S02 and redistribution permissions for all three sources have not been independently verified. The extracted text may still contain formatting or reading-order artefacts, so the prepared records should be checked against the original documents when used to support answers.

## 6. Data Lineage and Manifest

A Part B lineage manifest was generated to record the relationship between the original CloudJoi PDF documents and the prepared output.

The manifest, `data/week3_manifest.json`, contains three lineage records, one for each original PDF source. The records include the source and prepared-output file information, SHA-256 checksums and processing method.

All three original PDF documents were registered against the same prepared output, `data/processed/prepared_text.jsonl`.

The saved manifest was checked to confirm that the registered source and output files existed and that their SHA-256 checksums matched the recorded values.

A separate pipeline manifest was also generated during Part C at `data/pipeline_v1/documents/pipeline_manifest.json`. It records information about the executable V1 pipeline run and its validation results.

**Known limitations:** The publication versions and redistribution permissions of the original sources have not been independently verified. Successful checksum validation confirms file identity, not content quality, legal permission or complete reproducibility. The pipeline repeatability test confirms that the tested pipeline produced matching fingerprints and output information when rerun with the same inputs and configuration. It does not establish that the source documents will remain unchanged or that their contents are always accurate or current.
